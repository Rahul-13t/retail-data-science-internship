# Week 5 - Retail Pricing Strategy Optimization

This week focuses on developing a data-driven pricing strategy using historical retail transaction data.

## Work Performed

- Prepared historical retail transaction data
- Calculated product-level price, demand, and revenue
- Estimated price elasticity of demand
- Applied log-log linear regression
- Simulated different product prices
- Estimated expected demand for different prices
- Calculated expected revenue
- Identified a model-based optimal price
- Created price-demand and price-revenue visualizations
- Compared historical and optimized pricing
- Developed a pricing recommendation
